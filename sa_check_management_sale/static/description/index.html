Check Management - Sales

Stop selling on credit to customers whose checks bounced, and count checks that are not collected yet in the customer's credit limit.

Installs automatically with Sales Warn or block Credit limit aware

What it does

Bounced checks on sales orders

When the customer (or any contact of the same company) has incoming checks that are Bounced or under Legal Action, the quotation shows a red banner with the number of checks and the total. With the Block confirmation policy, only a Check Manager can confirm the order.

Open checks in the credit limit

A post-dated check settles the invoice when you receive it, so the receivable drops before the money reaches the bank. This add-on adds those uncollected checks (received, under collection or discounted) back into Odoo's credit-limit warning, and says so in the message.

A quotation for a customer with an unresolved bounced check

How to set it up

  1. Have Check Management and Sales installed. This add-on installs itself; nothing else to install.
  2. Go to Check Management → Configuration → Settings, block Sales.
  3. Bounced Checks on Sales:
    • Do nothing: no warning.
    • Warn on sales orders (default): a red banner on the quotation; anyone can still confirm.
    • Block confirmation: the banner, and Confirm is refused with the same message for everyone except Check Managers.
  4. Open Checks in Credit Limit (on by default): include uncollected customer checks in the credit used. It works together with Odoo's own customer credit limit (Accounting → Configuration → Settings → Customer Credit Limit, and a limit on the customer).

Good to know

  • Only payment checks count. Guarantee and security checks are ignored for both controls.
  • A bounced check stops counting as soon as it leaves Bounced / Legal Action: re-deposited, replaced, settled or returned.
  • Checks are looked up for the customer's whole company (all its contacts), in the order's company.
  • Only checks whose receipt entry is posted count toward the credit limit (with settlement at collection the invoice is still open, so it already counts).

Technical notes

  • Depends on sa_check_management and sale; auto_install. Version 18.0.1.2.0, license OPL-1.
  • Extends sale.order.action_confirm and account.move._build_credit_warning_message.
