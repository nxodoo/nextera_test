Check Management

Incoming and outgoing checks for Odoo 18: post-dated checks (PDC), deposits, collection, bounces, endorsement, bank discounting, guarantees, custody, checkbooks and multi-stage approvals, with every accounting entry posted and reconciled for you.

The suite at a glance

This is the core module. Two optional add-ons build on it: Check Management - Check Printing prints outgoing checks on the bank leaf, and Check Management - Sales (installs automatically with Sales) protects sales from customers with bounced checks.

What you get

Incoming checks

Receive post-dated customer checks, link them to invoices, deposit them in batches, record collection or bounce, re-deposit, take legal action, replace or settle.

Outgoing checks

Write vendor checks from registered checkbooks, route them through approval stages, issue, deliver and clear them, and handle stop payments, bank rejections and stale checks.

Automatic accounting

Checks Receivable / Payable, under-collection, discount and bank-fee entries are posted and reconciled automatically. A bank statement match closes the check by itself.

Endorsement and discounting

Endorse a customer check to a vendor against their bills, or discount it at a bank using per-bank advance rate, interest and fees.

Guarantees and custody

Hold guarantee and security checks with expiry alerts and optional off-balance memorandum entries. Track who physically holds every check with confirmed handovers.

Control and audit

Role-based permissions, approval of sensitive actions, duplicate detection, locked fields after Draft, an immutable history per check, daily reminders with escalation.

Getting started in 5 steps

1. Install

Install Check Management from Apps. The company needs a chart of accounts first. On install, and again on the first posting if anything is missing, the module creates what it needs: the Checks Receivable (PDC) and Checks Payable (PDC) accounts, a Checks Portfolio journal and the Check payment methods. You can replace any of them with your own accounts in the settings.

2. Give people a role

Open Settings → Users & Companies → Users, pick a user and tick their role in the Check Management section. Each role includes the one below it; Finance Approver, Check Accountant and Check Auditor are separate roles you add alongside.

3. Review the settings

Check Management → Configuration → Settings. The defaults work out of the box; these are the choices worth making on day one:

SettingWhat it decides
Invoice SettlementAt receipt / issue (default): the check settles the invoice the moment you receive or issue it, and the amount sits on a PDC account until the bank moves the money. At collection / clearing: the invoice stays open until the bank collects or clears the check.
Check Accounts / Checks PortfolioThe holding accounts and journal used for checks in hand and checks issued.
Checks Under CollectionPost an extra entry when checks are deposited, moving them to an under collection account.
Bounce Fees / Bank Charges AccountWhether a bank fee on a bounce is a company expense or charged to the customer.
Check DiscountingEnable discounting post-dated checks at banks, with the liability and charges accounts.
Re-deposit LimitHow many times a bounced check can be re-deposited before a Check Manager must do it.
Handover Confirmation / Default LocationWhether the receiver must accept a custody handover, and where new checks are held.
Approval Required / Segregation of Duties / Check ValidityOutgoing checks need approval before issue; people cannot approve checks they created; checks older than N months are flagged stale.
Foreign Currency ValuationThe rate date used to value foreign-currency checks in reports (receipt, due or collection date). Entries always use the rate of their own date.
Reminders / Escalation / Guarantee MemorandumReminder horizons in days, escalation to Check Managers, and off-balance entries for guarantee checks.

4. Prepare the master data

Everything below lives under Check Management → Configuration (Check Manager role):

  • Bounce Reasons: already loaded (insufficient funds, signature mismatch, account closed, payment stopped...). Add your bank's codes.
  • Custody Locations: safes, branches and people that physically hold checks, each with a responsible user.
  • Approval Stages: the approval steps for outgoing checks by amount range and purpose, each with its approver group. Without stages, one Finance Approval by the Finance Approver group is used.
  • Sensitive Action Rules: actions such as cancelling after Draft, marking a check lost or releasing a guarantee that must be approved by a chosen group before they run.
  • Bank Discount Terms: for each bank, the advance percentage, annual interest and fixed fee per check.
  • Checkbooks (Operations → Checkbooks): register each checkbook with its number range (or Import Leaves for a list of leaf numbers) and click Start Using. The bank then requires a leaf on every outgoing check.

5. Bring in the checks you already have

Configuration → Import Legacy Checks: click Download Template, fill one row per check (direction, number, partner, amount, dates, bank, drawer account, purpose, current state, journal...), use Check File to validate it, then Import. Each check lands directly in its real state (received, under collection, bounced, issued or delivered) with one Imported history event. Untick Post Opening Check Entries if your opening balances already contain the checks.

Daily work: incoming checks

  1. Create the check: Operations → Incoming Checks → New. Enter the check number, customer, amount, issue and due dates, the drawer's bank and account number (used to catch duplicates). Untick Drawn by Partner for a third-party check and type the drawer's name.
  2. Add the invoices it pays: in the Allocations tab click Add Invoices, tick the invoices and adjust amounts. The amount is spread over installments, earliest due first. Anything left unallocated becomes an advance.
  3. Receive: the check is posted, the invoices are settled (with the default policy) and the paper goes to the default custody location. Identity fields are locked from now on.
  4. Deposit: select one or more received checks in the list, or open one, and click Deposit. Choose the bank and slip number. A deposit groups the checks; print the Deposit Slip for the bank.
  5. Record the bank result: on the deposit (or the check) click Collect or Bounce. A bounce asks for the reason, the bank reference, an optional bank fee and the bank's notice as evidence. The customer's balance is reopened automatically.
  6. Follow up a bounce: Re-deposit, Legal Action (case number, lawyer, court), Replace with a new check (open allocations move to the new one), Settled Otherwise (link the payment that settled it) or Return to Partner.

Special cases

  • Endorse a received check to a vendor: choose the vendor and tick the bills it pays. If the vendor later returns it bounced, use Endorsed Check Bounced; if they return it unused, Returned by Endorsee.
  • Discount at Bank: pick the bank's discount terms; the advance, charges and net amount are computed from the days to maturity. At maturity record Collect or Bounce on the check.
  • Guarantee / security checks: set the purpose, the expiry date and what it guarantees. They never settle invoices. At the end use Release Guarantee (give it back) or Invoke Guarantee (it becomes a payment check).
  • Lost, Cancel and Reset to Draft are available with the right role, and may go through an approval request.

Daily work: outgoing checks

  1. Create: Operations → Outgoing Checks → New. Choose the vendor, amount, due date and bank journal, then the Checkbook Leaf; the check number fills in from the leaf.
  2. Add Bills in the Allocations tab, as for incoming checks.
  3. Submit for Approval: the approval stages that match the amount and purpose are created. Each approver clicks Approve (or Reject with a reason) in turn. With segregation on, nobody can approve a check they created, submitted or already approved at an earlier stage.
  4. Print Check (with the Check Printing add-on), then Issue: the bill is settled and the leaf is marked used.
  5. Deliver (who received it), optionally Mark Presented, then Mark Cleared when the bank pays it. Matching the bank statement line clears it automatically.
  6. Exceptions: Stop Payment, Rejected by Bank (then Replace or Settled Otherwise), Lost, and Void Stale Check once it passes its validity.

Custody, approvals and history

  • Handovers (Operations → Handovers): move checks from one holder to another. With confirmation on, the receiver must Accept or Refuse with a reason; print the Handover Receipt. Depositing a check moves it to the bank automatically.
  • Approval Requests (Operations → Approval Requests): when a Treasury Officer runs a sensitive action covered by a rule, a request is created and the approvers get a to-do. The approver clicks Approve and Run (the action executes) or Reject with a note. Requesters cannot approve their own request.
  • History: every step (state change, accounting entry, handover, approval, print) is written to an immutable log on the check and in Reports → Check History.
  • Accounting watch: if someone resets, cancels or unreconciles a check entry from Accounting, the check is flagged Needs Accounting Review. Use Repair Allocations to re-match it and Mark Accounting Reviewed to clear the flag.

Accounting, done for you

You never post a check entry by hand. Each button posts the right entry and reconciles it. Reversals keep the original entry and add a reversal, so the audit trail stays intact.

Dashboard, reports and reminders

  • Dashboard: outstanding, due today / 7 / 30 days, under collection, bounced, awaiting approval, guarantees held, collection rate and the expected cash flow. Click any figure to open the checks behind it.
  • Reports: Expected Cash Flow, Check Maturity (aging buckets), Bounce Analysis, Legal Cases and Check History, all as pivot / graph / list.
  • Printed documents: Check Voucher, Deposit Slip, Handover Receipt, Legal Case Sheet, and a Check Statement per customer or vendor (from the partner form).
  • Reminders: a daily job creates to-dos for checks due soon, matured checks not deposited, bounces to follow up, expiring guarantees, pending approvals, deposits without results and handovers not accepted, and escalates to Check Managers after the configured days.

Technical notes

  • Depends on account_accountant and mail. Version 18.0.1.14.0, license OPL-1.
  • The state of a check only changes through its buttons; each transition locks the record, checks the user's role and logs an event. Two users cannot process the same check at the same time.
  • Duplicate check numbers are blocked per bank and drawer account (incoming) or per bank journal (outgoing), ignoring spaces, dashes and leading zeros. A Check Manager can approve a legitimate duplicate.
  • Record rules keep every model inside the user's allowed companies.
  • Arabic translation included (i18n/ar.po).
