Overview

Training Needs Assessment helps HR and management teams identify, track, and fulfil employee training requirements through a structured workflow. Training requests can originate from KPI appraisals, direct manager requests, or compliance requirements.

Each training assignment flows through a kanban-friendly stage pipeline with assignable approvers, email notifications, and survey-based feedback collection. Training plans group multiple approved assignments into consolidated schedules with computed participant counts, manhour, and budget roll-ups.

Assignment Features

  • KPI Appraisal, Manager Request, and Compliance source types
  • Individual, Department-wise, and All Employees requirement categories
  • Auto-computed manhour based on participant count and duration
  • Internal and external trainer assignment
  • Venue selection from work locations
  • Survey link for post-training feedback collection
  • Email notification to trainees on approval
  • Approver assignment with email approval request

Stage Workflow

  • Draft → Submitted → Approved → Ongoing → Completed → Closed
  • Kanban view with drag-and-drop stage management
  • Assignable approver restricts stage transitions
  • Auto-schedule sync moves records to Ongoing/Completed based on dates
  • Statusbar widget on form for quick stage visibility
  • Protection against deleting system stages

Training Plan

  • Auto-populated from approved assignments matching date range and company
  • Computed totals: training count, participants, duration, manhour, budget
  • Automatic schedule state sync based on start and end dates
  • Calendar view for visual planning
  • Sequential serial numbering (TP/YYYY/xxx)

Attendance Tracking

  • One-click attendance sheet generation from training assignments
  • Auto-populated trainee list based on requirement category
  • Present/absent toggle per trainee
  • Computed present and absent counts
  • Facilitator tracking per session
  • Duration recording per attendance session

Email Notifications

The module sends automated emails at key workflow milestones:

  • Approval Request: When a training assignment is submitted, the assigned approver receives an email with a direct link to the form view.
  • Trainee Notification: When approved, all target trainees receive an email with training title, reference number, and schedule details.
  • Survey Invitation: Managers can send post-training feedback surveys to all participants with a single click from the completed form.

How To Use

  1. Go to Training → Training Assignment and click New.
  2. Enter the training title, select the source type (KPI Appraisal, Manager Request, or Compliance), and choose the requirement category.
  3. Select target employees or departments based on the requirement category.
  4. Set trainer type (internal/external), venue, dates, duration, and budget.
  5. Assign an Approver who will receive an email notification upon submission.
  6. Click Submit. The approver gets an email with a link to the record.
  7. The approver clicks Approve to move the assignment to the scheduled stage.
  8. Trainees automatically receive an approval notification email.
  9. Record attendance under the Training Attendance menu when training takes place.
  10. Use Training Plan to group approved assignments into consolidated schedules.
  11. After training completion, click Send Survey Mail to collect participant feedback.

Installation

  1. Download the module from the Odoo App Store.
  2. Place the bs_training_assessment folder in your Odoo addons directory.
  3. Restart the Odoo server and update the Apps list.
  4. Go to Settings → Apps, search for Training Needs Assessment, and click Install.
  5. Assign HR Manager access to relevant users for full functionality.
  6. Refresh the browser if the Training menu is not visible immediately.
Requirements: Odoo 18.0 with standard hr, hr_contract, and survey modules installed.
